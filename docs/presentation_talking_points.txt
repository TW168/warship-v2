SLIDE 2 — VOLUME: WHAT WE MOVE
--------------------------------
"Year to date, January through May, we shipped 59.8 million pounds. Same five
months last year we shipped 54.6 million pounds. That is a 9.5 percent increase
year over year. We are moving more product in 2026 than we did in 2025. The
challenge on the next slide is what that growth is actually costing us."


SLIDE 3 — SHIPMENT SIZE & WORKLOAD PRESSURE
--------------------------------------------
"Even though volume is up 9.5%, our workload did NOT go up by 9.5%. It is
actually more manageable this year because the average shipment got bigger.
In 2025 we were averaging 20,905 pounds per load. This year we are at 22,449
pounds per load — that is 7.4% larger loads. Bigger loads mean fewer truck
appointments for the same tonnage. This is the right direction."


SLIDE 4 — FREIGHT COST: WHAT IT COSTS
---------------------------------------
"This is where we have a problem. Freight cost in 2026 is running at 8.58 cents
per pound. In 2025 it was 7.68 cents per pound. That is an 11.7% increase in
cost year over year, on top of a 9.5% volume increase. We are shipping more
AND paying more per pound to do it. The next slide shows us exactly which
products are driving that number up."


SLIDE 5 — FREIGHT CENTS/LB DRIVERS: WHY IT MOVED
--------------------------------------------------
"Three products are responsible for the biggest freight cost spikes this year:

  - PSB0381501476AM-144P jumped from 4.79 to 9.98 cents per pound — that is
    plus 108.5%. More than doubled.
  - HCB0701801500AM-64 went from 5.64 to 8.76 cents — up 55.4%.
  - HSB0631601500AM-64 went from 9.61 to 12.58 cents — up 30.9%.

On the other side, three products got cheaper:
  - EHB0632955000AM-25 dropped 26.7%, from 8.68 to 6.36 cents.
  - PSB0331501476ENV-36 dropped 19.4%.
  - EPB0802006000AM-40 dropped 15.2%.

The question we need to answer for the top risers: is it the carrier charging
more, or did we shift that product to an expensive carrier? Those are two very
different fixes."


SLIDE 6 — CARRIER CONCENTRATION & COST
----------------------------------------
"We used 1,772 loads YTD across our carrier panel. GILTNER handles 555 of those
— that is 31.3% of all loads. AAL is second at 25.4%. Together those two carriers
handle more than half our freight. That is manageable but worth watching — if
either one has a capacity problem in peak season, we feel it immediately.

On cost: DRAKE is our most expensive real carrier at 14.15 cents per pound on
157 loads. For context, GILTNER runs more than three times the volume. That is
where a renegotiation conversation belongs."


SLIDE 7 — CUSTOMER MIX: WHERE IT GOES
---------------------------------------
"Year to date, our number one customer is WESTERN PLASTICS at 6.04 million

BAUMRUCKER, LEGACY PAPER, and WESTERN PLASTICS CA — are all between 1.38 and
1.44 million pounds each.

The concern: WESTERN PLASTICS alone represents nearly 48% of our top-five
customer weight. If that account slows down, we feel it. We need to keep that
relationship tight and keep growing the middle of the book."

month that is exactly where we want to be — intake and output tracking together."
