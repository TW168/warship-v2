Vision Learning

Scan barcode labels from pictures or sampled video frames, with decoded values and marked locations.

Module 1: Find a barcode in a picture

Upload a picture and the server will scan it for barcodes. The app does not retain uploaded pictures. If you access Warship over HTTP, uploads are not encrypted in transit.

JPEG, PNG, WebP, BMP, TIFF, or GIF. Maximum 10 MB.

Picture preview

Select the barcode area

Automatic scanning could not decode a barcode in the full picture. Drag a box around one barcode label and scan that area as a fallback.

Detected barcodes

Upload a picture and choose “Find barcode” to see results.

Scan a video for barcodes

The browser reads the video locally and sends one frame every half second to the barcode scanner. The video file itself is not uploaded or stored. Detected barcode areas are marked on example frames. Video is limited to 2 minutes; supported formats depend on your browser.

Frames are sent to the server for barcode decoding. HTTP connections do not encrypt them in transit.
